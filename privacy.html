Orqavian Gold - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

Who we are

Orqavian Gold is an app published by Orqavian (orqavian.com). Contact: hello@orqavian.com.

What data we collect

The app does not require an account and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts or payment details. We do not collect personal data through the app.

The app stores the most recent list of trade ideas on your device (local storage) so it can show them offline. This data stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Technical data

When the app loads trade ideas, your device requests a file from our web host. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and operation. We do not use this to identify you or to show advertising.

Advertising and third parties

The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not sell or share personal data.

Children

The app is not intended for anyone under 18 and trading information is not suitable for minors.

Not financial advice

Content in the app is for education and information only. It is not financial, investment or trading advice. Trading gold and leveraged products carries a high risk of loss. Orqavian does not execute trades or hold funds.

Your rights and deletion

Because we do not collect personal data in the app, there is no account to delete. You can clear locally stored data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. For any privacy question, email hello@orqavian.com.

Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
